## Narrowed pilot decision

Keep four functional written outcomes. Use **Serbian Latin only** for the first private draft; Cyrillic and script transfer are deferred. Use one primary target per scored activity and retain separate meaning and form judgments. Reserve one independent immediate assessment family per outcome. A 7- or 28-day result cannot be claimed until separately authored and protected families exist. All sequence edges remain editorial suggestions, never mastery prerequisites.

## Family allocation for authoring

These IDs are **reserved slots, not authored items or validated holdouts**. Each input, practice and assessment slot requires a distinct source/template/semantic cluster within the same taught target. Swapping names, numbers, script or synonyms does not create a new family. A shared target construction is expected; independence concerns the communicative situation and task template.

Before an assessment item is called unseen, freeze its source ancestry, template and semantic cluster, and check each learner's exposure to its prompt, key, translation and close paraphrases. Keep candidate assessment keys private; a repository filename alone cannot protect them. No delayed-probe slot is allocated here. Author independent immediate, day-7 and day-28 families plus replacements before any repeated-probe protocol is approved.

## Required record for each future item

- Identity: immutable item revision, outcome, one primary target, role, family, source ancestry, task template, semantic cluster and written Latin condition.
- Public side: fictional situation and addressee, Serbian stimulus if needed, Russian instruction that does not reveal a Serbian reading answer, response type, and any visible support. A production item must not display its model response before first submission.
- Private side: answer-policy revision, accepted forms or numeric key, item-specific incorrect and borderline examples, normalization/case/punctuation policy, meaning-success rubric, target-form rubric, reveal timing and support handling. Plausible unlisted free responses remain **unresolved**, not automatically wrong.
- Governance: accurate AI/human authorship and reviewer identity, Serbian text and Russian instruction/translation rights decisions with evidence, bilingual review decisions, unresolved defects and exposure history. A placeholder is not permission or human sign-off.

The next authoring pass must populate and review these slots in a **separate candidate pack**, keeping the eight P0 synthetic examples untouched. A validator can check IDs, required fields, script consistency, exact duplicates and declared family separation. It cannot certify natural Serbian, translation, semantic independence, rights or CEFR suitability. No runtime activity, learner progression or public release follows from this contract.
