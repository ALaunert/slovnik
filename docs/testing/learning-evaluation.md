# Learning evaluation contract (P0-01)

Status: definitions and synthetic baseline examples, 2026-09-25. This document does not change the API, database, scoring, scheduler, or learner-facing reports. The values in [the fixture](../../backend/tests/fixtures/learning/p0_01_histories.json) are specification examples, not observed outcomes or validated proficiency estimates. The [implementation plan](../learning/implementation-plan.md) assigns later reporting and diagnostic changes to separate tasks.

## Unit and observation rules

An **offered activity** is a recorded invitation to one learner for one target, capability, modality, and versioned context family. A **response** is a submitted answer, not an opened card or a completed page. Count one first response per activity, using its immutable first submission before feedback; later edits, final responses, duplicate delivery and idempotent replays cannot replace it. A retry is a separately linked activity. An objectively **resolved** response has a frozen answer policy and an accepted `correct` or rejected `incorrect` decision. `Unresolved` means a submitted candidate could not be decided under that policy; it is neither correct nor incorrect. A self-rating has no objective outcome.

For future measurement, record the learner, target/capability/modality, issued content and answer-policy revisions, context-family ID, offer and response timestamps in UTC, first response, support shown **before** that response (hint, reveal, correction), retry parent, evaluation source and outcome, and probe invitation/horizon. The ordinary task prompt and its intended cue are part of the issued task; an additional hint, answer reveal or correction is support. A blank hint list is evidence of no support only if the collecting flow actually records every support action. A new context family must be editorially distinct from prior practice and holdouts; changing an ID while reusing a prompt does not create independence. These are requirements for interpreting future data, not assertions that legacy rows contain them.

Deduplicate presentations by display ID, activities by issued ID, and responses by first accepted submission/idempotency key. Calculate per learner and target/capability/modality before aggregating. Publish counts alongside ratios and stratify at least by task/capability, support type, context family, answer-policy version and observation window. A ratio with denominator zero is **N/A**, never 0%. Missing, unclassifiable and unresolved records remain visible as separate counts; do not fill them with failures or successes. Historical rows without support or context provenance cannot be silently classified as unaided or new-context evidence.

## Metric definitions

In this table `N/D` is an operational fraction, not a mastery percentage. “First” is first submitted response to an issued activity, and “independent” additionally means no prior response to that context family for the same learner and target/capability/modality. A previous hinted answer or exposure of that family also prevents a later presentation of the same family from being called independent.

| Metric | Numerator / denominator | Include and exclude | Missing-data rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Material shown (exposure coverage)** | `N`: unique offered material displays confirmed rendered. `D`: unique offered material displays with confirmed `shown` or `not_shown` status. | Count material or answer actually made visible, including a new-word card, feedback reveal or correction. Exclude mere selection, a prompt that withholds the answer, duplicate display receipts and unissued content. A show gives contact evidence only. | Unknown render status is outside `D` and reported as `display_unknown` beside all offers. A recorded offer with confirmed `not_shown` stays in `D` and out of `N`. A legacy “complete” call is only a completion proxy, not proof of rendering. |
| **First independent unaided scored success** | `N`: correct first responses. `D`: correct + incorrect first responses meeting all eligibility rules. | First attempt for a learner–target/capability/modality–context family; no hint, reveal, correction or earlier response/exposure of that family; frozen, objectively resolvable key. Include wrong first answers in `D` even if a later retry succeeds. Exclude retries, self-ratings, supported responses, repeated families and unresolved responses. | Report eligible offers, submitted, resolved, unresolved, support/context unknown and unanswered counts separately. Do not turn a missing first response into failure; do not infer “unaided” from absent legacy hint telemetry. |
| **Assisted first-response success** | `N`: correct resolved first responses after support. `D`: correct + incorrect resolved first responses after support. | Support must be timestamped before the first response; report hint, full reveal and correction separately. Exclude retries and self-ratings; a copied revealed answer is supported practice, not independent retrieval. | Unknown support timing/status is unclassifiable, not “no hint.” Unanswered and unresolved supported offers are reported separately, outside `D`. |
| **Retry success** | `N`: correct resolved retry responses. `D`: correct + incorrect resolved submitted retry responses. | Require an explicit link to an earlier completed attempt for the same issued target and context. Count each permitted retry once, even if the first response was wrong. Exclude an unlinked new activity and duplicate submissions. Report retry offers and unanswered retries too. | Missing parent link makes retry status unknown; do not promote it to a new first attempt. Unanswered and unresolved retries stay outside `D` with separate counts. A correct retry never changes the first-attempt numerator. |
| **Subjective self-rating** | `N`: submitted `good` or `easy` ratings (or separately reported legacy `remembered`). `D`: all submitted valid self-ratings (`again`, `hard`, `good`, `easy` for review). | Count a rating only as the learner's report after its own review/self-check activity. Exclude it from every objective correctness numerator and denominator, regardless of `good`, `easy` or legacy quiz `is_correct`. Keep rating vocabulary and source separate when reporting. | No submitted rating is `missing_rating`, not `again`. An absent historical rating cannot be reconstructed from the current streak or due date. |
| **Delayed unaided new-context success, 7/28 days** | For each prespecified horizon, `N`: correct resolved eligible probe first responses; `D`: correct + incorrect resolved eligible probe first responses. **Also report** `invited`, `completed`, `unresolved` and `missing` probe counts. | Probe is offered at least 7×24 hours after the latest verified target contact for the 7-day band, or 28×24 hours for the 28-day band. Use `[7,28)` and `[28,∞)` day bands so a response is not counted twice. Require a new held-out context family, no support, a first attempt and a frozen key. Exclude same-family practice, retries, ratings and responses after feedback. | A probe invitation without a response remains in `invited` and `missing`, outside `D`; an ambiguous submission remains `unresolved`. Unknown last contact, context lineage, support or key makes eligibility unknown and cannot enter either band. Report missingness as `missing/invited` and resolved coverage as `D/invited` alongside success `N/D`. |
| **Unresolved-answer share** | `N`: submitted objectively scorable candidates explicitly left unresolved by the answer policy. `D`: all submitted scored response candidates with known type, including assisted answers and retries, whether resolved or unresolved. | Include ambiguity or valid-but-unlisted variants pending a policy decision; exclude self-ratings, mere exposures and unanswered offers. Preserve the candidate and reason under the applicable data policy. A model-only suggestion is not a final correct/incorrect decision. | Missing response text or key provenance is `unclassifiable`, separately reported rather than invented as an ambiguous answer or a wrong answer. Missing submissions are outside `D`. Never turn `unresolved` into an incorrect result by default. |

**Repeated-context first responses** are a companion diagnostic: `N` is correct and `D` is correct + incorrect resolved first responses on a previously used context family, excluding retries and supported answers. They are reported separately from the independent first-response fraction. Unknown family history is unclassifiable, and unanswered or unresolved repeats are reported separately. A new day alone does not make the same context independent.

The first-response and delayed fractions are **conditional on resolved submissions**. Their offer/invitation, completion, unresolved and missing counts are required companion denominators so attrition and scoring exclusions cannot be hidden by the conditional fraction. A future evaluation protocol must fix its population, content revisions, windows and analysis before inspecting outcomes. No useful-effect threshold or CEFR attainment rule is established here.

## What Slovnik actually stores today

| Existing path | Persisted facts | What the proposed metrics cannot recover reliably |
|---|---|---|
| New-word and review (`learning_service.py`, `shadow_learning_service.py`) | `UserWordProgress` stores seen timestamps/status, review interval/streak/due date and weak status. Completion records selected new words. When shadow mode is enabled, new-word completion writes an exposure event; a review rating writes a `self_report` event with `unknown` outcome and changes memory scheduling. | No verified browser rendering, pre-rating typed recall answer, full hint/reveal chronology, stable context family or independent success from a Good/Easy rating. The UI's reveal-first review is still a self-assessment. |
| Quiz (`quiz_service.py`, `shadow_quiz_service.py`) | `QuizAttempt` keeps a JSON question plan, completion time, `score` and total questions. Ordered `QuizAnswer` rows keep prompt, submitted answer, binary `is_correct` and timestamp; an incorrect question can be answered once more. Enabled shadow mode links first and retry activities, preserving a first response and deterministic or self-report evaluation events. | Old plans lack frozen grading keys/context families. Grading and mistake corrections read mutable vocabulary. Hint timing and held-out family are not captured. The self-check's `remembered` maps to a Good-like shadow rating with `unknown` objective outcome. Existing binary grading has no unresolved alternative. Shadow mode is off by default and cannot backfill missing records. |
| Progress projection (`learner_projection_service.py`, `memory_policy.py`) | `EvidenceSummary.count` counts events; `deterministic_count` counts deterministic responses. `CompetenceEstimate.success_weight` and `failure_weight` increment on **every** deterministic correct/incorrect response, including a retry; `peak` is an uncalibrated monotonic heuristic. Self-ratings affect memory scheduling, not competence weights. | It does not filter hints, retries, repeated families or delayed held-out probes. `peak`, confidence-like fields and scheduler intervals are not an observed probability or a CEFR level. |

The current **quiz practice score** is reported as `score` out of `total_questions`: a question receives credit when **any** of its up to two answers is marked correct. Its fraction, if calculated, is `score / total_questions`. This combines first answers, repaired retries and the `remembered` self-check with objective quiz questions. Keep its existing API label/behavior for now, but call it a *mixed practice score*, never a mastery percentage or first-attempt accuracy. The legacy `correct_count` also includes correct retries, and an old `QuizAnswer.is_correct` reflects the then-current binary scorer, not an independently validated answer key. No historical delayed transfer or CEFR attainment claim follows from these data.
